# Decision 001 — Payments are processed outside Mirent

## Date
2025

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Mirent operates in the Philippine market where integrating
with GCash, Maya, or bank APIs requires BSP licensing,
significant legal compliance, and high development cost.
For V1, this adds unnecessary risk and delay.

## Decision
Mirent will NOT process, hold, or transfer any payments.
All payments between guests and hosts happen outside the platform
(GCash, bank transfer, cash). Mirent only records payment
proof uploaded by agents and confirmation by hosts.

## Consequences
- Faster V1 launch (no payment gateway integration)
- No BSP licensing required for V1
- Host bears full responsibility for payment verification
- Receipts generated by Mirent are reference documents only,
  not official payment confirmations
- Future V5+ may revisit in-app payments with proper licensing

## Alternatives considered
- GCash API integration — rejected (BSP compliance too complex for V1)
- Stripe integration — rejected (not widely used in PH market)
- Escrow system — rejected (requires financial entity license)
